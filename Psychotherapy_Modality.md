# Psychotherapy Modality

## Overview

A psychotherapy modality is a defined therapeutic approach, framework, or method used in psychotherapy.

## Examples

- Cognitive behavioral therapy
- Psychodynamic psychotherapy
- Humanistic psychotherapy
- Systemic therapy
- Integrative psychotherapy
