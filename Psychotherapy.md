# Psychotherapy

## Overview

Psychotherapy refers to structured psychological interventions intended to help people address emotional, behavioral, interpersonal, or other psychological difficulties.

## Major Approaches

- Cognitive behavioral approaches
- Psychodynamic approaches
- Humanistic approaches
- Systemic approaches
- Integrative approaches
