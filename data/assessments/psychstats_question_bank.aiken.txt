According to DSM-5-TR and WHO ICD-11 diagnostic criteria, what is the minimum duration of excessive, uncontrollable worry required to establish a diagnosis of Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD)?
A. At least 6 months, occurring more days than not
B. At least 2 weeks, accompanied by depressed mood
C. At least 1 month following an identifiable acute stressor
D. At least 12 months with intermittent panic attacks
ANSWER: A

In first-line Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) protocols for panic disorder, what is the primary neurobehavioral mechanism underlying interoceptive exposure?
A. Extinction of catastrophic misinterpretations via systematic habituation to somatic sensations
B. Immediate suppression of autonomic arousal through deep diaphragmatic avoidance
C. Desensitization of unconscious psychodynamic defense mechanisms
D. Pharmacodynamic downregulation of postsynaptic serotonin receptors
ANSWER: A
