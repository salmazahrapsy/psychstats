# Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs

## Overview

Maslow's hierarchy of needs is a motivational framework associated with psychologist Abraham Maslow. It is commonly presented as a hierarchy involving physiological needs, safety, love and belonging, esteem, and self-actualization.

## Key Concepts

- Physiological needs
- Safety needs
- Love and belonging
- Esteem
- Self-actualization

## Historical Context

Maslow introduced his theory of human motivation in the 1940s and later expanded his discussion of human motivation.

## Important Context

The popular pyramid representation should not be treated as a complete summary of the empirical literature on human motivation. Contemporary psychology uses a range of motivational theories and empirical approaches.
